# Analysis of AI in the American Journal of Neuroradiology

## 1. Executive Summary

This bibliometric study analyzes 250 papers published in the American Journal of Neuroradiology between 2017 and 2026. The total number of citations across these papers is 5,627, with a mean of 22.51 citations per paper. The total citations age-adjusted to account for the varying publication dates is 941.0. The study highlights the growth and impact of various subfields within AI in neuroradiology, identifies the most-cited papers, and discusses emerging trends and influential authors and institutions.
