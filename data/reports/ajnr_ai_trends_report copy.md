# Analysis of AI in the American Journal of Neuroradiology

## 1. Executive Summary

This bibliometric study examines the evolution of AI research in the American Journal of Neuroradiology from 2017 to 2026. Over this period, the journal has published 288 papers, with a total of 15,604 citations. The most prominent subfields include Detection/Classification (CADx), Radiomics/Quantitative Imaging, and Image Segmentation. Key trends include the increasing focus on Explainability/Trustworthy AI and the growing importance of Large Language Models/NLP. The most cited papers often integrate multiple subfields, particularly CADx, Radiomics, and Explainability. Influential authors and institutions are predominantly from the United States, with Stanford University and Harvard Medical School leading in both the number of publications and citations.
